# Device Setup Fails During Company Enrolment: What You Can Do

If your company setup does not complete during device enrolment, your files are still safe.

## Try these steps first
1. Keep the device connected to the internet (wired or trusted Wi-Fi).
2. Restart the device once.
3. Sign in again and retry the company setup process.
4. Open Settings > Accounts > Access work or school.
5. If you see multiple old work/school connections, do not remove them yourself unless IT tells you to. Take a screenshot instead.
6. Wait for IT instructions before attempting more retries.

## What not to worry about
- This issue does not mean your personal files were deleted.
- This issue does not automatically mean your account is compromised.
- The problem is usually a device management registration conflict.

## Information to send to support
Please share these details so IT can resolve it faster:
- Your full name and work username.
- Device name (for example DESKTOP-FB099).
- Exact error text if shown (for example "already enrolled in MDM").
- Time of last failed attempt.
- Screenshot of Settings > Accounts > Access work or school.
- Whether one restart and one retry were completed.

Contact the Service Desk if setup still fails after one restart and one retry.
