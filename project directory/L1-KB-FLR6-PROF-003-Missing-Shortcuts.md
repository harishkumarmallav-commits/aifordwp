## Problem: Desktop Shortcuts Are Gone

**Symptom:** Your desktop doesn't show the usual icons/shortcuts for Outlook, Word, Excel, etc.

**Good News:** Your files are completely safe. The shortcuts are just links—even if they're gone, everything still works.

**Time to Fix:** 5 minutes (if you do it yourself)

---

## Quick Fix #1: Check If They're Just Hidden (Try First!)

Some shortcuts might just be hidden, not deleted.

**Step 1:** Click View menu (top of any open File Explorer window)  
**Step 2:** Look for "Hidden items" checkbox  
**Step 3:** Check it  

**Do your shortcuts appear?** You're done! They were just hidden.

---

## Quick Fix #2: Check Recycle Bin

Your shortcuts might be in trash.

**Step 1:** Double-click Recycle Bin (or search for it)  
**Step 2:** Look for files ending in `.lnk` (these are shortcuts)  
**Step 3:** Right-click them → Restore  

**Did you find them?** You're done!

---

## Quick Fix #3: Recreate Them (Super Easy - 5 Min)

You can create new shortcuts in one minute.

**For any app (Outlook, Word, Teams, etc.):**

1. Click Start button (Windows logo)
2. Type the app name (e.g., "Outlook")
3. Right-click the app → "Send to" → "Desktop (create shortcut)"
4. A new shortcut appears on your desktop
5. Repeat for each app you need

That's it! Takes 30 seconds per app.

## Why Did This Happen?

When the new document management app was deployed Friday, something went wrong with the desktop customization. Likely causes:
- App setup accidentally cleared desktop
- Desktop settings got reset to default
- Hidden Group Policy change affected desktop display

**This is NOT:**
- A virus or security problem
- Data loss (your files are safe)
- Permanent (you can always recreate or restore)

## FAQ

**Q: Are my files gone?**  
A: No! Only the shortcuts (links) are gone. All your actual files are perfectly safe.

**Q: Do I need to restart my computer?**  
A: Not usually. Try the quick fixes first. If that doesn't work, a restart might help.

**Q: Will this happen again?**  
A: Unlikely. We're improving our deployment testing to prevent this.

**Q: Why wasn't I told about this earlier?**  
A: This was a low-priority issue (annoying but not work-stopping). Your login and file access were working normally. The high-priority issue was the login failures, which were fixed first.

**Q: How long will this take to fix?**  
A: 5 minutes if you do it yourself. Up to 1 hour if you wait for IT to restore from backup.
