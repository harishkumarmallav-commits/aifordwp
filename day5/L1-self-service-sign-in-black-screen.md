# Black Screen When You Log In: What You Can Do

If you see a black screen when you log in, your files and company data are safe.

## Try these steps first
1. Wait for up to 60 seconds on the black screen.
2. If nothing changes, sign out from the log-in screen.
3. Wait 2 minutes.
4. Sign in again.
5. If it still happens, restart your computer once and sign in again.

## What not to worry about
- Your documents, email, and saved work are not deleted by this issue.
- This is a log-in access problem, not a data loss problem.

## If you need to contact support
Please include these details so the IT support team can help faster:
- Your full name and work login name.
- The exact time the issue started.
- What you saw (for example: black screen, then sign-out, or black screen that did not clear).
- Whether it worked after waiting 60 seconds.
- Whether it worked after signing out and back in.
- Whether it worked after one restart.
- Your computer name (if known) and office or location.

Contact the IT support team if the black screen happens again after these steps, or if you cannot log in at all.
