# Triage Assessment - Copilot Support Tickets

**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Analyst:** DWP engineer triage draft

## Approach
- Causes are ranked most probable first using only the allowed categories.
- Non-Copilot explanations are preferred unless the ticket text rules them out.
- "Genuine Copilot fault" is kept as the last resort in every ranking.

## Ticket Assessments

## Quick Patterns Across These Tickets
- Tickets 1, 3, 6, and 8 primarily read as access-scope problems rather than Copilot defects.
- Tickets 2 and 7 are most efficiently triaged through provisioning, indexing, and client readiness checks.
- Ticket 4 is best explained by external sharing limits.
- Ticket 5 is the only one that could plausibly escalate toward a broader service issue, but only after licensing, policy, and client-state checks are ruled out.
