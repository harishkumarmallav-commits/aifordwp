# End-User Communications - Copilot Support Tickets

**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Purpose:** Plain English user-facing responses for each ticket, with the next likely step.

## Ticket 1
**User issue:** Copilot will not summarise the Q3 board pack in SharePoint even though the user can see the file.

**End-user communication:**
Hi, thanks for reporting this. The most likely reason is that Copilot cannot use that board pack in the same way you can open it manually. This can happen if the file has tighter permissions or protection settings behind the scenes. The file itself may still be available to you in SharePoint, but Copilot may not be allowed to use it for summarising.

**Next step:**
We will check the file's permissions and protection settings first. If needed, we may ask you to confirm the exact file location so we can test the right document.

## Ticket 2
**User issue:** A new hire says Copilot in Outlook does not seem to know about their recent emails.

**End-user communication:**
Hi, thanks for flagging this. As you only started very recently, the most likely explanation is that your mailbox content has not fully caught up in the Microsoft 365 background systems that Copilot relies on. This can happen for newly created users, even when email itself is working normally.

**Next step:**
We will first confirm when your mailbox and Copilot access were set up. If this is just normal background delay, the issue should improve once indexing completes.

## Ticket 3
**User issue:** An HR manager asked Copilot in Word to use a sensitive salary review spreadsheet and got an access message.

**End-user communication:**
Hi, thanks for the report. The message you received usually means Copilot is not allowed to use that spreadsheet, even if you expected to have access to it. Because this is salary review data, it may also have extra protection applied to it.

**Next step:**
We will check your effective access to that spreadsheet and whether any protection label is preventing Copilot from using it.

## Ticket 4
**User issue:** Copilot in Teams cannot find a client contract that was shared through a guest link from another organisation.

**End-user communication:**
Hi, thanks for reporting this. The most likely reason is that the contract was shared from outside your organisation, and Copilot may not be able to use externally shared guest content in the same way it uses content stored in your own organisation.

**Next step:**
We will confirm whether the contract is only available through an external guest share. If so, the likely workaround is to use a version stored within the correct internal location, subject to normal access controls.

## Ticket 5
**User issue:** Copilot stopped working for the whole Finance team this morning after working yesterday.

**End-user communication:**
Hi, thanks for raising this. Because this affects a whole team at the same time, the first checks will focus on service setup rather than individual user error. This could be related to licensing, policy, or client changes rather than a fault with your files.

**Next step:**
We will check whether anything changed this morning for the Finance team's Copilot access or client setup. If those checks are clear, we will then assess whether this needs to be escalated as a wider service issue.

## Ticket 6
**User issue:** Copilot found and summarised a file the manager had forgotten they could access.

**End-user communication:**
Hi, thanks for checking this. What you described is usually expected behaviour. Copilot can use files that you already have permission to access, even if you have not opened them recently or had forgotten they were in a folder available to you.

**Next step:**
We will confirm the access on that folder and file. If you no longer need access, your local file owner or access manager may need to review permissions.

## Ticket 7
**User issue:** Copilot gives generic answers and does not seem to use internal SharePoint content.

**End-user communication:**
Hi, thanks for reporting this. When Copilot gives very general responses, it often means it is not grounding its answer in your work content. The first things to check are whether your Copilot access is active and whether you are using it from the correct signed-in work account and supported app.

**Next step:**
We will verify your Copilot licence and client sign-in state first. If those are correct, we will then check whether your SharePoint content is still catching up in the background systems Copilot uses.

## Ticket 8
**User issue:** An executive assistant cannot get Copilot in Outlook to see a shared mailbox calendar they manage for a director.

**End-user communication:**
Hi, thanks for flagging this. Managing a shared or delegated mailbox calendar does not always mean Copilot can use that calendar as part of your own Outlook context. In many cases, shared mailbox access and Copilot access do not behave the same way.

**Next step:**
We will confirm whether this is a shared or delegated mailbox scenario and check whether that mailbox type is supported for the action you were trying to use.
