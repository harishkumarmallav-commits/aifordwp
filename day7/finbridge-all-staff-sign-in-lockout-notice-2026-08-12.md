# FinBridge: Sign-in and account lockout notice

We are aware that some users are having sign-in and account lockout issues this morning, and the service desk is already working on it.

In the meantime, if you are affected, please try signing out fully and signing back in once more. Check that your password is entered correctly and that Caps Lock is off. If you recently changed your password, wait a few minutes and try again.

We will share another update by the end of the morning, or sooner if we have new information to share.

Please stay calm and avoid repeated sign-in attempts, as that can make the issue worse. If you still cannot get in after trying the steps above, contact the service desk with your username, the exact message shown, the time it happened, and how many times you tried.
