### Excel crashing on large spreadsheets

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Status** | **New — emerging, high priority** |
| **Affected group** | Finance team and users working with large files (>10 MB) |
| **Volume** | 9 comments across 40 (IDs 4, 8, 13, 16, 20, 24, 30, 33, 37) |

**Why it is new:** No week 1 triage ticket, RCA, or feedback reference mentions Excel stability issues. This appears post-migration or post-upgrade.

**Supporting evidence from week 2:**

- ID 24: "Excel large-file crash happened again, please look into this urgently."
- ID 30: "Excel keeps crashing, this is now my biggest issue with the new setup."
- ID 33: "New Excel crash issue — happens specifically with files over 10MB."
- ID 37: "Excel crash is new and quite disruptive, please prioritise."

**Pattern:** Crashes are consistently linked to large files (explicitly >10 MB in ID 33). Finance team is disproportionately affected given their large budget spreadsheets. Data loss risk is confirmed (ID 13: "losing unsaved work each time"). This warrants an urgent triage ticket.
