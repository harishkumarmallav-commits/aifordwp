# Leadership Update — FinBridge Migration: End-User Experience

**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Prepared by:** DWP Service Desk  
**Audience:** Non-technical management

---

The migration has landed well for the majority of staff. The issues that affected users most in the first week — difficulty logging in, account lockouts, files appearing missing, and VPN problems — have all been resolved, and user feedback in week two confirms this clearly. People are logging in reliably, their files are where they expect them to be, and remote access is stable. The overall tone of staff feedback has shifted noticeably from frustration to satisfaction, with many users proactively noting the improvement. For most of the organisation, day-to-day work is back to normal or better than it was before the migration.

Two issues remain open and are being actively worked. First, the third-floor printer has not been restored since the migration began and staff in that area have now been without it for two weeks; frustration is high and the risk of formal escalation is real — this needs to be prioritised and a firm resolution date communicated to affected staff. Second, Finance team members have begun reporting that Excel is freezing and crashing when working on their larger spreadsheets, with some losing unsaved work as a result; this did not exist before the migration and is now their most pressing day-to-day problem. In addition, a security configuration update applied to Finance devices on 4 August has caused login times to roughly double for that group — from around 18 seconds to over 40 seconds — and while this is not causing data loss, it is a visible daily friction for 215 people. Investigation is under way on all three items and updates will follow as findings are confirmed.
